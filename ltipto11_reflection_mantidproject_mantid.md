# Reflection: mantidproject_mantid

activity pattern: it rose then declined, it's huge with 176,744 commits from 530 authors, activity grew from 2007 up to about 17-18k commits a year in 2016 to 2018, dipped after 2019, came back up in 2021, then has been around 6-8k a year since 2022. There is only 1 gap of 3 months or more and it's 4 months from 2007-05 to 2007-08.

gaps: this was easy to figure out because it's the planning phase not inactivity. The only commit before it was "Create initial directories" in April 2007, and the old tickets show they spent the summer on requirements docs, meeting minutes, and an architectural design doc.

recovery: coding started in Sept 2007 when Russell Taylor committed the algorithm interface, algorithm factory, makefiles, and LoadRaw, all tied to the design tickets, so a new person started the actual code after the planning team finished

now: it's very active with commits every day in Oct 2026 getting ready for release 7.0
