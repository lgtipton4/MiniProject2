# Reflection: mic-dkfz_nnunet

activity pattern: it's steady, there were 2575 commits from 245 authors and it has had commits every month since April 2019 except one. It's usually 200 to 600 commits a year with peaks in 2022 to 2024 around nnU-Net v2. There are 0 gaps of 3 months or more, the longest is just 1 month in 2020-06.

gaps: this isn't really a gap so I put N/A active project. Commits stopped on 2020-05-28 and started again on 2020-07-06, but 25 issues were opened in June 2020 and 24 got closed by the end of July, so the maintainer was busy answering users instead of pushing code.

recovery: the commits right after fixed nnUNet_find_best_configuration and cleaned up ensembling, which is what people were asking about in the June issues. It was the same person the whole time (Fabian Isensee) with some outside contributors here and there

now: it's very active with multiple commits a week in Sept and Oct 2026
