# Reflection: cambridgemolecularengineering_tabledataextractor

activity pattern: it declined, there were 318 commits from 10 authors, 275 of them came between Oct 2018 and Dec 2019 while the tool and its paper were being made, then it dropped to about 18 commits a year in 2020 and 2021. There are 3 gaps of 3 months or more and the longest is 37 months from 2022-01 to 2025-01.

gaps: this one was easy to figure out. The last PR before the gap came from a branch called jm2111_post_publication and was just "Minor clean-up", so the paper was out and the tool was basically done. The main author JurajMa never committed again, and an install issue from Nov 2021 is still open with no answer, so nobody was really watching the repo.

recovery: only partial, there were 6 commits in Feb and Mar 2025 from a new person in the same Cambridge group (Dingyun Huang) that updated versions and pinned numpy below 2.0, then Taketomo Isazawa merged it and bumped the version. It was probably numpy 2.0 breaking installs that brought them back.

now: it's inactive, the last commit was on 2025-03-14 and nothing came after the dependency fix
