# Reflection: r-spatial_gstat

activity pattern: it's irregular, there were 538 commits from 20 authors but it's mostly one maintainer Edzer Pebesma. It's usually low activity tied to CRAN releases with one big burst in 2021 (185 commits) and smaller ones in 2016, 2019 and 2024. There are 4 gaps of 3 months or more and the longest is 6 months from 2023-05 to 2023-10.

gaps: this was easy to figure out. The last commits before the gap were "fixes for CRAN release 2.1-1" and "bump version after CRAN release" in April 2023, so it had just been released and nothing needed doing. The maintainer also works on a lot of other r-spatial packages, and issues people opened during the gap never got answered

recovery: it came back because of the ecosystem, rgdal, rgeos, and maptools got retired from CRAN in Oct 2023, and in Nov 2023 the maintainer opened an issue to update the demos for that and started committing again. A new person  also added a pkgdown website in Jan 2024, so it was the same maintainer plus one new contributor

now: it's active and the last commit was 2026-04-29
